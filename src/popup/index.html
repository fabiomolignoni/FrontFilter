Interface

Scroll limit

Comment limit

Counts the comments that reply to the post, each shown with its replies. Comments hidden by filters do not count.

Blocked subreddits

HOME blocks only the subreddit's front page, with its hot, new, top and rising views.

ALL blocks every page of the subreddit and hides its posts from Home and Popular.

Use * as a wildcard, as in news* for every subreddit whose name starts with “news”.

To add several at once, paste a list, one per line or separated by commas.

Allowed subreddits

These subreddits override blocked subreddit rules and “All subreddit front pages”.

To add several at once, paste a list, one per line or separated by commas.

Blocked users

Hides their posts and comments everywhere on Reddit, along with the replies to their comments, and blocks their profile pages.

To add several at once, paste a list, one per line or separated by commas.

Keywords

Hides posts and comments whose title, text preview or comment text contains one of these as whole words: ai hides “AI art”, not “said”. Replies to a hidden comment are hidden too.

Add * to an end to match inside words too, as in spoiler* or *ai*. Matching ignores case and accents.

For other matches, write a regular expression between slashes, as in /colou?r/.

To add several at once, paste a list, one per line or separated by commas.

Allowed keywords

Keyword filters never hide a post or comment that contains one of these. They match as keywords do; other filters still apply.

To add several at once, paste a list, one per line or separated by commas.

Post flairs

Hides posts with these flairs, ignoring case. Use * as a wildcard, as in *politic*.

To add several at once, paste a list, one per line or separated by commas.
